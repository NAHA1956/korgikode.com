What it does

A fuel log that keeps the useful numbers together.

Log every fill-up

Record fuel amount, total cost, odometer reading, fuel type, date, and notes so your vehicle history stays organized in one place.

Track MPG and fuel costs

Use your fill-up history to follow fuel economy, mileage, cost per mile, and other useful statistics instead of relying on scattered receipts.

Remember where you filled up

Clara's Fuel Log can keep location information with an entry, making it easier to see where a fill-up happened and review it later on a map.

Review and export your history

Browse past fuel entries, look at trends and charts, and export your data to CSV when you want a copy outside the app.

Clara's Fuel Log is built for drivers who want a straightforward iPhone fuel mileage log without turning a basic task into a complicated project. Add the fill-up, keep the history, and let the numbers become useful over time.
