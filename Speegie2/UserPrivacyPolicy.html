User Privacy

Effective August 27, 2026

Speegie2 is designed to keep your measurements and calculations simple and under your control.

Your Measurements

Specific-gravity values, repeated measurements, comparison results, notes, and other test information you enter are used to provide the app’s calculation and history features.

No Advertising Profile

Korgi Kode does not use your test information to build advertising profiles and does not sell your test data.

Your Responsibility

Speegie2 is a calculation and record-keeping tool. You are responsible for verifying measurements and deciding how results are used in your own quality-control or testing process.

Deleting Information

You can remove records using the features provided by the app. Copies contained in system backups may remain according to your device and backup settings.
