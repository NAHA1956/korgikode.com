Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Korgi Kode respects your privacy. Speegie2 is a specific-gravity calculation and quality-control utility designed to work with measurements you enter.

The app may store measurements, test values, dates, notes, and related calculation information that you choose to enter.

Your information is used to perform calculations, compare measurements, display results, and maintain any history provided by the app.

Speegie2 does not require you to create a Korgi Kode account and is not designed to collect personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third Parties

Korgi Kode does not sell or rent your Speegie2 data to third parties.

Storage

App data is stored using Apple platform technologies. System backups or Apple services may handle copies of app data according to your device settings and Apple’s policies.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version will remain available on the Korgi Kode website.
