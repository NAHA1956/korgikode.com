KorgiFolderFlair Support

Need help with KorgiFolderFlair? We're here for you.

How do I apply flair to a folder?

Simply open the app, select the icon or flair you want to use, and drag your target folder into the KorgiFolderFlair window. The custom icon will be applied instantly.

How do I remove flair from a folder?

Select the modified folder in Finder, press Command + I (or right-click and choose "Get Info"). Click the small folder icon in the top-left corner of the Info window so it is highlighted, and press the Delete key on your keyboard.
