User Privacy Commitment

KorgiFolderFlair is designed to respect your privacy by default. We believe your data belongs to you.

  • No Accounts: You do not need to create an account or sign in to use the app.
  • Local Processing: All folder customizations are processed locally on your Apple device.
  • No Cloud Syncing: We do not sync, read, or store any of your folder names, structures, or contents on our servers.

If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to korgi.kode@yahoo.com.
