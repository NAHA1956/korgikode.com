Clara’s Home Inventory

User Privacy

Effective August 27, 2026

Clara’s Home Inventory is built around a simple principle: your home inventory belongs to you.

Your Data

Box records, item records, categories, notes, photos, QR-label information, backups, and other content you create are used to provide the app’s features.

No Advertising Profile

Korgi Kode does not use your inventory to build advertising profiles and does not sell your inventory information.

You Control What You Enter

You decide what information is stored in the app. Avoid entering highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable storing it on your device or through any Apple services you have enabled.

Exports and Sharing

If you export, print, share, or back up inventory information, you are responsible for the resulting files and where they are saved or sent.

Deleting Information

You can remove inventory records through the app. Copies stored through system backups or Apple services may be subject to those services’ retention behavior.

Questions

For questions, contact korgi.kode@yahoo.com.
