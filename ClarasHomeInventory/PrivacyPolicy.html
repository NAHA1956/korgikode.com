Clara’s Home Inventory

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Korgi Kode respects your privacy. Clara’s Home Inventory is designed to help you organize personal storage boxes and the items inside them.

Information You Enter

The app may store information you choose to enter, including box names, item descriptions, categories, storage locations, notes, photographs, and QR-code information.

How Information Is Used

Your information is used only to provide the inventory, organization, search, label, backup, restore, and related features of the app. Korgi Kode does not sell your personal information or use your inventory data for advertising.

Photos

The app accesses photos only when you choose to add a photo to an inventory record. It does not independently scan or upload your photo library.

Storage and Apple Services

The app uses Apple platform technologies to store and manage its information. If Apple services such as iCloud are used, those services are also subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policies.

Exports and Backups

If you export, print, share, back up, or restore inventory information, you control the destination and use of those files.

Third Parties

Korgi Kode does not sell or rent your inventory information to third parties.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version will remain available on the Korgi Kode website.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact korgi.kode@yahoo.com.
