About Us

Useful software with a little corgi spirit.

Korgi Kode is an independent app studio creating practical tools for Apple platforms. Our apps begin with everyday problems: finding what was packed away, tracking a vehicle, preparing App Store screenshots, or keeping music moving on a road trip.

Clara, our corgi inspiration, gives the studio its personality. Each app is built to stay approachable, respect the user’s privacy, and do its job without unnecessary complexity.
