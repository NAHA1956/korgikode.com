Clara’s EV Charging Log

Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Korgi Kode respects your privacy. Clara’s EV Charging Log helps you maintain a personal record of electric-vehicle charging sessions.

Information You Enter

The app may store charging dates, charging type, energy, cost, odometer readings, battery percentages, charger information, notes, and electricity-rate settings.

Location Information

When you grant permission, location may be used to find nearby public chargers and record a charging location. It is used for app functionality, not advertising.

Calculations

Home energy and cost calculations are estimates based on the battery capacity, percentage change, electricity rates, and time settings you provide.

Apple Services

The app may use Apple services for location, maps, Siri, CarPlay, and storage. Those services are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policies.

Advertising and Sale of Data

Korgi Kode does not sell your charging or location information and does not use it to create an advertising profile.
