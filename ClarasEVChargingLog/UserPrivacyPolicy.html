User Privacy

Effective September 4, 2026

Your EV charging history is intended to remain under your control.

Your Charging Records

Charging sessions, costs, odometer readings, battery percentages, notes, and calculated summaries are used to provide the app’s logging and reporting features.

Your Location

Location is used only for features you choose, such as locating a public charger or recording where a charging session occurred.

Estimates

Estimated energy and cost values are clearly dependent on the vehicle and electricity-rate information you provide and may differ from utility or charging-network bills.

No Advertising Profile

Korgi Kode does not sell your charging history or use it for targeted advertising.
