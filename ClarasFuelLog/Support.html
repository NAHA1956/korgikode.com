Before Contacting Support

Please make sure you are using the latest available version of the app and the latest compatible version of macOS or iOS for your device.

Reporting a Problem

When contacting support, please include the app name, app version, device type, operating-system version, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Screenshots are welcome as long as they do not contain private information.
