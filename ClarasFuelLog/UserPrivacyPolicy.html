Clara’s Fuel Log

User Privacy

Effective August 27, 2026

Clara’s Fuel Log is intended to keep your personal vehicle and fueling records under your control.

Your Fuel Records

Fuel purchases, costs, odometer readings, fuel types, notes, and calculated statistics are used to provide the app’s logging and reporting features.

Your Location

Location information is used only when needed for features you choose to use, such as recording or displaying a location associated with a fuel entry.

No Advertising Profile

Korgi Kode does not use your fuel history or location history to create advertising profiles and does not sell that information.

You Control Sharing

When you export or share a fuel log, you control the destination. Exported records may contain vehicle, cost, mileage, or location information.

Deleting Information

You can remove fuel records through the app. Copies stored through device backups or Apple services may be subject to those services’ retention behavior.

Questions
