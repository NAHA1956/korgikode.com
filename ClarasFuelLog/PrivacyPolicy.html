Clara’s Fuel Log

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Korgi Kode respects your privacy. Clara’s Fuel Log is designed to help you keep a personal record of fuel purchases, mileage, fuel economy, and related vehicle information.

Information You Enter

The app may store information you choose to enter, including dates, fuel type, fuel amount, cost, odometer readings, notes, and other fuel-log information.

Location Information

When you grant location permission, the app may use your device location to help identify or record the location associated with a fuel entry. Location information is used for app functionality and not for advertising.

How Information Is Used

Your information is used to calculate and display mileage, MPG, costs, statistics, history, maps, and exports. Korgi Kode does not sell your fuel-log or location information.

Apple Services

Apple platform services may be used for location, mapping, storage, or other system features. Those services are subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policies.

Exports

If you export or share fuel-log information, the exported file may include information you entered in the app. You control where that information is saved or sent.

Third Parties

Korgi Kode does not sell or rent your fuel-log information to third parties.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version will remain available on the Korgi Kode website.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact korgi.kode@yahoo.com.
