User Privacy

Effective September 4, 2026

KorgiConvoy is designed to use only the information necessary to create and participate in a shared music experience.

Your Music

Your Apple Music account and playback access remain controlled through Apple’s system permissions. Korgi Kode does not receive your Apple Account password.

Your Convoy

People with the current convoy code may be able to join and participate. Share the code only with people you want in the group.

No Advertising Profile

Korgi Kode does not sell convoy activity, listening activity, or song requests and does not use them for targeted advertising.

Your Choices

You may leave a convoy, stop playback, or revoke Apple Music access through your device settings.
