KorgiConvoy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Korgi Kode respects your privacy. KorgiConvoy helps people in a traveling group share an Apple Music playlist and coordinate playback.

Information Used by the App

The app may process a temporary convoy code, playlist selections, playback state, and song requests needed to connect participating devices.

Apple Music

KorgiConvoy uses Apple Music features with your permission. Music availability, account access, subscriptions, and playback are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policies.

Convoy Coordination

Temporary coordination data may be exchanged through an online service so participating devices can join a convoy, share playlist changes, and coordinate playback. Do not use convoy names or codes to share sensitive information.

Advertising and Sale of Data

Korgi Kode does not sell your personal information and does not use convoy activity to create an advertising profile.

Changes

This policy may be updated as KorgiConvoy changes. The current version will remain available on this website.

Contact

For privacy questions, email korgi.kode@yahoo.com.
