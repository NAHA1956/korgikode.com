User Privacy Choices for Clara’s Screenshot Sizer

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

Clara’s Screenshot Sizer does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or track personal information.

Because the app does not collect user data, there are no data-sharing, tracking, advertising, or account-related privacy settings to manage.

Your Screenshots

All screenshot processing occurs locally on your Mac.

The app accesses only the images you choose to import and saves processed files only in the export location you select.

Managing Your Files

You remain in control of all imported and exported screenshots. You can view, move, rename, or delete exported files at any time using Finder.

Data Access and Deletion Requests

Korgi Kode does not maintain user accounts, screenshot copies, or personal-data records. Therefore, Korgi Kode has no stored user data to provide, correct, or delete.
