Privacy Policy for Clara’s Screenshot Sizer Effective Date: August 24, 2026 Korgi Kode respects your privacy. Clara’s Screenshot Sizer is designed to process screenshots locally on your Mac. Information We Collect Clara’s Screenshot Sizer does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies. Screenshot Processing Screenshots imported into Clara’s Screenshot Sizer are processed locally on your Mac. Your screenshots are not uploaded to Korgi Kode or transmitted to a remote server. File Access The app accesses only the screenshots you choose to import and the export location you select through the standard macOS file chooser. Processed screenshots are saved only to the folder you select. Korgi Kode does not have access to those files. Data Retention and Deletion Because Korgi Kode does not collect your screenshots or personal information, Korgi Kode does not retain any user data. You may delete imported or exported screenshots at any time using Finder. Third-Party Services Clara’s Screenshot Sizer does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or data-collection services. Children’s Privacy Clara’s Screenshot Sizer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Changes to This Privacy Policy This policy may be updated if the app’s features or privacy practices change. Any revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
